# Payback Privacy Policy

Effective: October 6, 2026

Payback is a skills-only AI agent plugin. It has no server, database, analytics service, account system, or external API.

## Data collection

Payback itself does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal data.

The Payback skill consists only of instructions that tell a compatible AI agent to adapt its communication style to the current user. Any conversation data processed by the host AI product is handled by that product under its own privacy terms and settings; Payback does not receive a separate copy.

## Cookies and tracking

Payback does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, telemetry, or cross-site tracking.

## Third parties

Payback does not send data to third-party services.

## Data retention and deletion

Because Payback itself stores no user data, it has no Payback-specific retention or deletion process. Users should use the controls provided by their AI host for conversation or account data.

## Changes

Material changes to Payback's data practices will be reflected in this policy and in the public source repository.
